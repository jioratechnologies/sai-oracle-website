(1) The Magic Of Lord’s Blessings :-

Astonishing Miracle of History:-
How baba showered his utmost blessing's on Gurumaa
 

Sai is infinite love . Sathya is what he teaches , Dharma is what he lives . Shanti is mark of His personality, Prema is His very nature . love of Sai has no words and love of Sai for Gurumaa is the relation filled with fragrance of Purity . 7th jan 2000 was one of the auspicious and one of the most memorable day, when Swami gave shaakshaatkaar gift to Gurumaa on her birthday.

It was around 5 pm in evening when as usual one of the Gurumaa devotee went in the Satyadeep Sai Universe to keep milk as Prasad for Swami . As she entered the premises of this Universe, she was astonished to see scattered vibhuti in the whole Sathyadeep Sai Universe . She saw that in front of each idol from Bhagwan Ganesh , Shirdi Sai baba,Lord Hanuman, Lord Durgamaa,Lord Radha- Krishna & Lord Shankar- Parvati, a nariyal and a lingam was also placed.

The whole temple glittred by the sparkling rays from these fascinating lingums . This lingodbhavam wonder was a golden period on the history of sai leela's , such lingum's were never seen on earth before this occasion . All these Narials &lingums are symbols of Swami’s unconditional & pure unsullied love for her beloved Gurumaa.

As she saw all this ,she just ran to call Gurumaa and when Gurumaa entered a voice echoed in everyone ears ,present in Sathyadeep Sai Universe "THIS IS GIFT FOR YOUR BELOVED GURUMAA FROM SWAMI ON THIS AUSPICIOUS OCCASSION". Today all these Beautifull Lingums and Nariyals are placed for Darshan in Serva Dharma Sathal in Sathyadeep Sai Universe.The vibhuti gifted by Swami to Gurumaa was distributed by Gurumaa among all devotees present in Sathyadeep Sai Universe as Swami prasad.
