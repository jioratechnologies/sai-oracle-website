Gurumaa Live Sketch
"Service to Mankind is Service to God"

Love all,Serve all.

Param Pujniye Gurumaa means a Master Mother" or "Divine Mother”,an enlightened Divine soul. Her unsullied love,her Divine Grace, her ways to remove agonies & sorrow’s, her Guided meditation techniques, her way how she connects everyone to Sai Baba,her ways to reach towards higher stages of Human consciousness and in-depth  Divine energies are some of triats of Param Pujinye Gurumaa.

An Symbol of Ahimsa, human values and universal brotherhood.Param Pujniye Gurumaa is one such spiritually blessed Divine soul among galaxy of divine souls,one such upcoming shining star who is teaching Mankind "Bhakti Yoga"(Unflenching love towards God),Karma Yoga"(Merits and Demerits of Good and Bad Deeds),"Jnana Yoga"(Supreme Knowledge of True Self),"Dhyana Yoga"(Meditation).

 

Param Pujniye Gurumaa was Born in Agra in north India, her life showed Miracolous symptoms of a Divine Powers inherited in her. Her unflenching and unsullied love towards Sai Baba and Her ways to Perform Nishkama Karma(Desireless actions) have motivated and Transformed the Hearts of thousands of innocent people towards Sai Baba. From very begining various spiritual incidents took place which strengthened her way to unite with God. Various sants and sages visit her house and showed special affection for this child.

 

Even as a young child, Param Pujniye Gurumaa was often found in deep meditation and at age of seven she surprised her parents by her deep meditations, many a times her parents shaked her, but unable to disturb her, as the body was there, but the soul was attached to Sai Baba, who took her soul and had made her Darshan’s of Vakunt and every spiritual Piligrims in India.

 

From the tender age of nine she had a divine darshan of bhagwan Shri Sai Baba, there after she through her extraordinary signs of Divine Powers and through her innocent offerings to Sai Baba, she connected herself with Lord Shiva. She often use to go in Manakamehwar temple early in morning everyday at 4 A.M with 100 liters of milk for Abhishekh of Lord Shva. She use to run in Vrindavan streets behind Lord Krishana. At the age of twelve she had Darshan of Lord Hanuman, Bhagwan Shri Satya Sai Baba, Shirdi Sai Baba at her home and is still carried by her till now.

 

 

Soon Param Pujniye Gurumaa started Delivering Discourses,and Propogated the glory of Bhagwan shri Sai Baba in such a amazing manner that People were drawn to her satsang,made themseleves a part of Divine Blessings Showered by Spiritually Blessed soul "Param Pujniye Gurumaa".Till Now Param Pujniye Gurumaa Through her Divine Presence has Transformed Thousands of Hearts Towards the Millenium Avatar Bhagwan Shri Sai Baba. Immense divine vibrations were surrounding the satsang hall in Her Divine Presence together with the Presence of Bhagwan Shri Sai Baba. Many people and children's started having their Guru’s Darshan in their dreams, where they were guided by their divine Gurumaa. Miracles started happening at her young age among her devotees, that strengthen their faith & proved the mystic presence.

 

Her formula for man to lead a meaningful life is the five fold path of Sathya (Truth) Dharma (Righteousness), Shanti ( Peace), Prema( Love) and Ahimsa( Non- violence). Love for God, fear of sin and morality in society, these are her prescriptions for our ailing world. She is a inexhaustible reservior of pure love.

 

Her message of “Brotherhood of man” and fatherhood of Good” is a spiritual salve that will lead mankind from the darkness of ignorance to the light of immortality.

 

Slowly and Slowly the fragrance of divine love and Nishkama Seva spreaded allaround and reached large number of people. Till now The Divine Flame Litted by her had reached all over the world. Thousands of people and young children’s are being transformed towards the inner path (Divine Path). Her greatest success lies behind that she had transformed thousands of young children towards the lotus feet of the Lord & receiving the divine bliss there after.

 

Those are Fortunate who are being guided by Bhagwan Shri Sai Baba and his Highly Blessed Soul"Gurumaa".
